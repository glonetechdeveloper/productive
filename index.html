Mastering the Top-Down Productivity Hierarchy

Productive is engineered on a fundamental principle: peak output is not about doing more random tasks, but aligning every single 60-minute block of your day directly with your 1-year master vision.

01

Hourly Time Blocks (Precision Micro-Scheduling)

Daily execution from 06:00 AM to 11:00 PM (18 discrete focus blocks)

The Time Blocks view is your daily command center. Rather than maintaining an endless disorganized to-do list, your active day is divided into 18 discrete 1-hour slots where every hour has a designated outcome and category:

Step 1 Set Primary Objective

Enter your single most critical non-negotiable outcome in the top objective banner.

Step 2 Assign Hourly Tasks & Tags

Click any slot to type your task and select category tags (Deep Work, Core Focus, Health, etc.).

Step 3 Toggle Execution State

Click the checkmark icon when completed, or cross icon if missed. Progress counters calculate in real-time.

Step 4 Routine Dropdown Actions

Click Routine ▾ to instantly 1-click apply named blueprints or launch the Routine Architect.

02

Daily Routine Blueprints & Routine Architect

Pre-configure, save, edit, and apply custom 18-hour daily schedules in seconds

Never re-type your schedule from scratch. The Routine Blueprints engine allows you to build pre-saved schedules for different operational cadences:

1-Click Routine Dropdown

Click the Routine ▾ button in the Time Blocks header to open the dropdown menu. Choose from built-in presets (Standard High-Performance, Deep Work Sprint, Ops & Strategy, Weekend Mastery) or any custom routine you have created. It instantly populates all 18 hours of the currently selected day.

Routine Architect & Builder Page

Click + Add New Routine to open the full Routine Architect page. Name your routine, write descriptions, and customize each of the 18 hourly slots with task names and category tags. Save your blueprint to use anytime on any day, or click Save & Apply to Today.

03

7-Day Matrix & Daily Execution Review Log

Sunday through Saturday holistic cadence and historical audit

Review the flow of your entire week in one glance. The 7-Day Matrix summarizes completion percentages across every day of the active week, with a historical audit log table tracking completed hours, missed blocks, and focus ratings.

  • Direct Day Jumper: Click any day card or click Open on the table to immediately jump to that specific day's hourly time block view.
  • Weekly Momentum Score: Monitors whether your scheduled hours balance effectively with actual completed output.
04

Weekly Strategy & High-Impact Big Rocks

Pre-week strategic intentions and top 5 weekly deliverables

Before launching into the week, top operators establish high-leverage intentions. Switch between Weeks 1 through 4 using the segmented control.

Strategic Intentions Notepad

Jot down your key focus, anticipated energy bottlenecks, risks, and non-negotiables. Auto-saves continuously to your database.

Big Rocks Task Feed

Track your top priority weekly milestones. Check them off as they get shipped or delete finished deliverables with the trash icon.

05

4-Month Horizon Planning

Bridging annual dreams with quarterly operational sprints

One year is too long to stay focused on a single sprint; one week is too short for massive compound shifts. The year is divided into three 4-month horizons:

Horizon 1 (M1 - M4) Foundation, setup & initial launch
Horizon 2 (M5 - M8) Scale, optimization & mid-year push
Horizon 3 (M9 - M12) Compounding victory & annual retrospective

Inside each month, define high-level strategic targets and add granular checkpoints with the + Add Checkpoint button.

06

Annual Vision & Master Goals

Your north star pillars, quantitative metrics, and cumulative tracker

Define high-level life and business pillars (Career & Wealth, Health & Endurance, Mastery, Engineering). Set concrete quantitative metrics (e.g. $250,000 ARR or Sub-4hr Marathon) and update their status (In Progress, Achieved, Deferred). The master progress bar computes cumulative completion automatically.

07

Cloud Sync, Data Safety & Keyboard Shortcuts

Zero data loss with local storage and PostgreSQL serverless cloud
Cloud & Local Synchronization

Works 100% offline out-of-the-box using browser Local Storage. When connected via the Sign In modal, data synchronizes seamlessly to PostgreSQL serverless cloud with stateless JWT security.

You can also export full JSON backups or restore backups at any time via the bottom footer.
